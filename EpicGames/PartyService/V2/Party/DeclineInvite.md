## Party Service V2 - Decline Invite

URL: https://api.epicgames.dev/epic/party/internal/v2/users/:accountId/invites/:inviterId <br/>
Method: DELETE <br/>
Auth Required: Yes (Account Auth)

## Path Parameters

`accountId`: Your AccountId <br/>
`inviterId`: AccountId of the inviter

---

*Example Response*: Status 204
